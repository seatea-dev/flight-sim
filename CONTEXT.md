# Flight Simulator

This context describes the player's flight around a small coastal island.

## Language

**Circuit**:
A short flight that starts with takeoff from the island runway, passes a route landmark, and returns to the same runway for a safe landing.
_Avoid_: Mission, level

**Landmark**:
A lighthouse on a rocky point that counts as passed when the aircraft comes within a generous distance of it.
_Avoid_: Checkpoint, gate

**Safe landing**:
A touchdown on the runway at a manageable speed and descent rate. Minor heading errors still count.
_Avoid_: Perfect landing

**Crash**:
A run-ending impact with water, terrain, or a solid structure, or a hard or off-runway touchdown.
_Avoid_: Stall
